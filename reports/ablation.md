Generated by `python -m lexbridge.eval.run` from `reports/runs/*.json`; do not edit.
Overall scores are macro means over the text-retrieval categories: each category weighs
the same, so hundreds of templated questions do not drown the hand-written ones. The map
lookups ("IPC 420 is now which section?") are a graph lookup, not a text search, and are
reported only in the per-category table. `wrong-regime@1` is the share of cases with a
counterpart in the other code whose first result is that counterpart (lower is better).
Topical cases are templated from section headings and overstate real-world quality; the
lay, Hindi, boundary and tricky columns are the hand-written test, and with 6-28 cases
each, one question moves them by 4-17 points.
